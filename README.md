SOLMIRA is a travel poster for a place that does not exist, made for make.fun's "The poster for a place that I..." bounty. The picture shows a white-domed desert city spread across a vast white salt flat at golden hour, with one tall mirrored-glass lighthouse tower catching the low sun, long shadows raking over the flat, and an amber-to-teal sky above it all, drawn in a vintage screen-print style. The only copy set into the picture is the place's own name, laid large over the sky.

main.ts hands the poster in by copying the committed entry.png to /output/entry.png, honoring MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR when it is set. The poster is 1024×1024 and well under 1 MiB, and the copy is byte-for-byte, so every run hands in exactly the same picture.
