**SOLMIRA** — a travel poster for a place that does not exist, built for
make.fun's "The poster for a place that I..." bounty.

A white-domed desert city on a vast white salt flat at golden hour, one
tall mirrored-glass lighthouse tower glowing in the low sun, long
shadows across the salt, an amber-to-teal sky. Vintage travel-poster
screen-print style, flat shapes, limited palette. The only copy in the
picture is the place's name, set large over the sky.

`main.ts` hands the poster in: it copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` (honoring `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` outside the grader).
The poster is 1024×1024 and under 1 MiB, and the copy is byte-for-byte,
so every run hands in the identical picture — no randomness, no
network, nothing to drift.
